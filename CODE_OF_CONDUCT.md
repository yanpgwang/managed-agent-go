# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be a focused, welcoming place to collaborate. Be
respectful, assume good intent, discuss ideas on their merits, and make space
for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained
personal attacks, deliberate disruption, sharing another person's private
information, or sexualized language and attention.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, issues, community
spaces, and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers or GitHub
reporting tools. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit
participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect the community.
Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.

Good-faith disagreement about technical decisions is welcome. Abusive behavior
is not.
