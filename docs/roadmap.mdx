---
title: "Roadmap"
description: "What Mango is stabilizing now and what comes later"
---

Mango is stabilizing the single-agent runtime before widening the product
surface.

## Now — close the alpha loop

- Tighten compatibility for Agents, Environments, Sessions, Events, and SSE.
- Finish File-backed outcome rubrics and endpoint-aware context limits.
- Validate post-compaction custom Skill behavior against the hosted platform.
- Promote remote sandbox adapters only after repeatable live conformance.

## Next — make it operable

- Authentication, authorization, and tenant isolation.
- Metrics, traces, audit logs, quotas, and operational runbooks.
- Explicit migrations, worker versioning, rolling-upgrade coverage, and
  versioned production Compose/Helm assets.
- Deployment-managed MCP authentication and private-network connectivity.
- Memory Stores, Memories, and Vault credentials.

## Later — expand the platform

- Multi-agent rosters, threads, delegation, and targeted interrupts.
- Deployments, schedules, and webhooks.

The [compatibility matrix](/compatibility) remains the source of truth for what
works today. Roadmap items are direction, not release commitments.
